Privacy Policy for Sundar Patro

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Sundar Patro ("we", "our", or "the app") is a standalone, offline Nepali Bikram Sambat calendar and panchang application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Sundar Patro is designed with complete user privacy in mind:

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, or location).
  • No Tracking or Analytics: The app contains no third-party trackers, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or telemetry tools.
  • Offline Local Storage: Any user preferences (such as selected language or theme mode) are stored strictly locally on your device using standard local web storage and are never sent to external servers.

2. Permissions

The app requires no device permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage). It does not request internet access: all calendar data and fonts are bundled inside the app, so it makes no network connections. Links you choose to open (for example, a website) open in your device's browser.

3. Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect any personal data, it is safe for users of all ages, including children.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository or support contact.
