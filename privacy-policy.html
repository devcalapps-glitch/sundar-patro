Privacy policy

Sundar Patro · Effective November 12, 2026

Sundar Patro is a Nepali Bikram Sambat calendar and panchang app made by Longest Chariot LLC. This policy covers the Sundar Patro Android app and these web pages.

Sundar Patro does not collect, store, or share any personal user or calendar data. The app displays banner advertisements from Google AdMob to fund maintenance.

What the app uses

The app bundles all calendar data, Bikram Sambat conversion tables, festival dates, tithis, and fonts, and runs them on your device. It saves your language and display theme preferences on your device in local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes those preferences. The app sends nothing you view or do in the calendar to our servers.

Sundar Patro does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or storage.

When you turn on Holiday reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and uses it only to remind you at 6 PM the evening before festivals and public holidays. The app calculates and schedules these reminders on your device without contacting a server. It also re-arms these reminders when your phone restarts. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app under More, or in your phone's notification settings.

Advertising and third-party services

Sundar Patro requires no user account or login. The app displays banner advertisements from Google AdMob to fund development and maintenance.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process technical data to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID and Android ID.
  • IP address and device network information.
  • App interactions and diagnostic logs, such as ad impressions and clicks.

The app transmits this data over HTTPS to Google under Google's Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies. For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving ads.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts these web pages. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site. We do not use this information to identify or track visitors. Read GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children's privacy

Sundar Patro is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date above.
