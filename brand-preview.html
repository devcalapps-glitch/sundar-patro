Brand Identity Concept

Sundar Patro (सुन्दर पात्रो)

Review the 3 visual directions below. Each logo represents a unique balance of cultural heritage and modern digital elegance.

Option 1 • Recommended

The Surya-Patro (सूर्य-पात्रो)

A harmonious fusion of a modern calendar tablet with celestial icons: the radiant Sun (Surya) representing solar Bikram Sambat dates, entwined with the subtle Crescent Moon (Chandra) for lunar Tithi and a vermilion Sindoor bindi.

Option 2 • Cultural Monogram

The Devanagari ‘सु’ (कलात्मक सु)

A bespoke typographic glyph for the letter ‘सु’ (Su for Sundar). The traditional Shirorekha acts as the top calendar binding, the glyph’s curve forms a rising morning sun, and a radiant Tika sits at the top right.

Option 3 • Architectural Minimal

The Minimal Focus Grid (बिन्दु र वृत्त)

An ultra-clean, architectural calendar grid inspired by modern utilities (Cal.com & Apple Human Interface). The 3×3 matrix highlights "Today" (आज) with an energetic vermilion focal ring surrounding a golden center dot.
