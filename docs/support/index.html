Frequently asked questions

Does Sundar Patro require internet access?

No. Sundar Patro is designed to be 100% offline. All calendar dates, Bikram Sambat to Gregorian conversions, tithis, and festival information are bundled directly inside the app.

Does the app collect any personal data?

No. Sundar Patro collects zero personal data, has no user accounts, and contains no ads or analytics trackers. Read our full Privacy Policy.

How do I switch languages or themes?

You can toggle between Nepali and English, or switch between light and dark modes, directly from the interface controls at any time. Your preference is saved locally on your device.

How accurate are the Bikram Sambat dates and festivals?

Sundar Patro utilizes precise astrological calculations and verified panchang algorithms for official Bikram Sambat calendar years, aligned with authentic Nepali calendar standards.
