Privacy Policy

Sundar Patro · Effective November 12, 2026

Sundar Patro is a Nepali Bikram Sambat calendar and panchang app, made by LONGEST CHARIOT LLC. This policy covers the Sundar Patro Android app and these web pages.

In short: Sundar Patro does not collect, store, or share any personal user or calendar data. We display banner advertisements served by Google AdMob to support the app.

What the app uses

All calendar data, Bikram Sambat conversion algorithms, festival dates, tithis, and fonts are built directly into the app and run locally on your device. The app remembers your selected preferences (such as language and display theme); this is saved only on your device using local storage and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Nothing you view or do in the calendar is ever sent to our servers.

Sundar Patro does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or storage.

If you turn on Holiday reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications and uses it only to remind you the evening before festivals and public holidays. These reminders are worked out and scheduled entirely on your device; no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. The app also re-arms these reminders when your phone restarts. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app under More, or in your phone's notification settings.

Advertising & Third-Party Services

Sundar Patro does not require user accounts or logins. To support ongoing development and maintenance, the app displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process certain technical data to serve ads, combat fraud, and measure ad performance, including:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / Android ID).
  • IP address and general device network information.
  • App interactions and diagnostic logs (such as ad impressions and clicks).

Data is transmitted securely via encryption in transit (HTTPS) and handled directly by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain required consent before serving ads.

Website hosting

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may briefly process standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site. We do not use this information to identify or track visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children's privacy

Sundar Patro is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
