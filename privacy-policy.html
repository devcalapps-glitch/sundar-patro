Privacy Policy

Sundar Patro · Effective November 10, 2026

Sundar Patro is an offline Nepali Bikram Sambat calendar and panchang app, made by LONGEST CHARIOT LLC. This policy covers the Sundar Patro Android app and these web pages.

In short: Sundar Patro does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app uses

All calendar data, Bikram Sambat conversion algorithms, festival dates, tithis, and fonts are built directly into the app and run locally on your device. The app remembers your selected preferences (such as language and display theme); this is saved only on your device using local storage and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Nothing you view or do in the app is ever sent to us or anyone else.

Sundar Patro does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos, or external storage, does not read device identifiers, and requires zero sensitive device permissions.

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

Sundar Patro has no sign-in or user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The app works fully offline and makes no external network requests.

Website hosting

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may briefly process standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site. We do not use this information to identify or track visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children's privacy

Sundar Patro is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
